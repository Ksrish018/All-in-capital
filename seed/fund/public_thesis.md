## Where the public record and the case differ

These differences matter for scoring. They are the start of the "stated versus revealed thesis" question from Meeting 5.

1. **Sectors.** The case lists four sectors: fintech, deeptech, consumer tech and consumer brands. The fund's own words say sector-agnostic, and its public portfolio includes B2B SaaS (MeetRecord, Rocketlane, Mailmodo), B2B marketplaces (Fashinza) and edtech (NPrep). So the sector check must not treat "outside the four" as a hard fail. It should be a soft signal, and Kushal decides how strict to be.
2. **Revenue.** The fund says most first cheques are pre-revenue. Several seed applicants already have revenue. Revenue is a plus, never a requirement. Missing revenue must not count against a pre-product company, but a missing number that the business should have (such as repeat rate for a selling consumer brand) still does, as Meeting 1 says.
3. **Response time.** The fund promises a reply in 3 days and a decision in about 10. Our 24 hour brief target fits inside this. The outreach timing (follow ups on day 3 and day 7) should be checked against this promise.
4. **Cheque size.** The stage check should use the current range ($250K to $500K first cheque, lead under $1M rounds) and not a fixed pre-seed label.
